Introducing DC Careers Connect A better way to find and share DC Government jobs

DC Careers Connect is the new home for DC Government career opportunities.
Instead of hunting across multiple agency websites, you—and the people you know—can now start in one place and explore roles across the District.

What's new:

  • One website to browse opportunities across DC agencies
  • Simple, broad job categories to help candidates find a good fit

Coming soon:

  • Employee video spotlights and career journeys
  • More agencies and roles added regularly

You can help:

Share dc.gov/jobs with your family, friends, and neighbors and help make DC government an employer of choice.
